Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Beamly Recorder ("the app") is made by BeamLab. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

Beamly Recorder does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

The app has no account system and does not send your data to BeamLab or any third party. It contains no ads and no analytics.

Data on your device

  • Screen recordings, screenshots and edited videos are saved only on your device, in your Movies and Pictures folders. You control them and can delete them at any time.
  • App settings are stored locally on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone: records audio into your screen recordings, only while a recording is running.
  • Camera: shows the optional face cam bubble on screen. Camera video is not uploaded.
  • Display over other apps: shows the floating record controls, countdown, brush and face cam.
  • Notifications: shows recording status and lets you stop a recording.
  • Screen capture: Android asks for your permission each time before the app can record or screenshot your screen.
  • Storage (Android 10 and below only): saves recordings and screenshots.
  • Audio files: files you pick for background music are read only to mix them into your video.

Deleting your data

Because all data stays on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time by deleting the files or uninstalling the app. BeamLab holds no copy of your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
